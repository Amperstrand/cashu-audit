## The mechanism problem (and the three failed fixes)

Upstream 2.0 removed the fork's entire custom-method platform
(register{Mint,Melt}Method, keyRingService access, deep
operations/{melt,mint} imports) and its types funneled merged methods
to never at four layers. The recon's plan — declaration merging into
"@cashu/coco-core" — is SOUND at the source level but DEFEATED BY
BUNDLING: tsdown/rolldown-dts attributes interfaces to hash-named
internal chunks, and consumer augmentation only merges the module it
targets. Failed in order: (1) deep-path retarget (paths gone),
(2) interface relocation into index.ts (roll still chunks them),
(3) leaf entry methods.ts (chunks whenever any other entry reaches it).

Working fix: the method registries (Mint/MeltMethodDefinitions +
Input/Quote variants) are **global interfaces** (methods.ts,
`declare global`), which merge BY NAME regardless of chunking, plus a
`_methodRegistryAnchor` re-export so the globals load in every consumer
program (side-effect imports are erased from emitted d.ts — the
silent failure that cost one debug round: the merge appeared to work
while consumers had created a parallel interface).
